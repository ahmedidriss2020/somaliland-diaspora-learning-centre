Dhaqan · Culture

Not only the language. The whole inheritance.

Every child on every track also learns where they come from — taariikh, gabay and maahmaah, woven through the reading, the writing and the conversation.

TaariikhHistory

Where the family comes from, told at a level a child can hold: the sultanates, the poets, the pastoral life, and the journey that brought their parents here.

GabayPoetry

Somalis are called a nation of poets. Children learn to hear the metre, memorise short verses, and recite them out loud without embarrassment.

MaahmaahSayings

The proverbs that carry how Somalis think. A child who knows fifty maahmaah understands far more than fifty sentences.
